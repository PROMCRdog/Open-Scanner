# Community code of conduct

Open Scanner contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and technically honest.

- Be considerate, specific, and evidence-led in reviews.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels without harassment or exclusion.
- Never post another person's network identifiers, device data, credentials, or private communications.
- Do not use the project community to coordinate intrusive scanning, exploitation, disruption, or credential attacks.
- Resolve disagreement around reproducible evidence, user safety, accessibility, and documented product boundaries.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated incidents should be reported privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`.
