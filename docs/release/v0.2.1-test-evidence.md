## Physical-device evidence

- An earlier v0.2.1 candidate upgraded in place from the permanent-signer v0.2.0 installation. During final emulator verification, an unconstrained connected-device test command inadvertently removed the physical-device package; the exact final candidate was therefore installed fresh. Any removed app-local preferences were not recoverable, and the exact final-candidate in-place-upgrade gate is open again.
- The exact final permanent-signed candidate installed on the authorized OPPO PMA120 on Android 16 / API 36 with version code 3 and version name 0.2.1. Pulling the installed base APK returned the same 2,042,125 bytes and SHA-256 as the candidate.
- A 5.8 GHz connected AP reported primary channel 149, 80 MHz width, and center frequency 5775 MHz. The chart kept the bonded footprint centered at 5775 MHz and placed the dashed primary marker at channel 149.
- A dense 2.4 GHz snapshot displayed 9/9 observed networks by default. The selector exposed the full scrollable list, Only focused reduced the view to one curve, and Show all restored adaptive all-network mode.
- Per-group selection survived Track-to-Spectrum tab switching while fresh scan totals changed, confirming the filter is group-scoped and adaptive rather than a stored four-item subset.
- Focus was changed from the connected channel-149 AP to the other observed channel-149 AP. The selector kept exactly one focus radio selected, left both curves visible, and retained the Current Wi-Fi badge on the connected AP.
- On the exact final candidate, the Simplified Chinese selector visibly labelled the column `焦点 · 单选`, outlined the focused row in cyan, displayed a cyan focus badge, and muted the unfocused radio without disabling it. Selecting that radio moved the badge and focus while the Current Wi-Fi badge stayed on the independently connected AP.
- The resulting chart showed the focused curve in cyan and the different current connection in green. When both roles were on one AP, the green peak marker kept connection status visible on the cyan focused curve.
- English labels, counts, focus controls, role legend, selector dialog, Current Wi-Fi badge, and channel-149 explanation remained readable at the device's default text scale; earlier Simplified Chinese coverage remains recorded for this candidate line.
- The physical Track picker also displayed the exact Current Wi-Fi badge on the connected AP while the focused row remained separate.
- No crash or ANR was recorded after exact-candidate installation and interaction. The final device state was restored to Show all on the 5.8 GHz group.
